## Research Findings (Research Agent)
All verified empirically against `Godot_v4.7.1-stable_win64_console.exe`, including against the real repo's shipped code (not synthetic probes) for the critical finding. Temp probes deleted; real repo untouched.

1. **`Engine.time_scale=0.0` — CONFIRMED REAL BUG, verdict (b):** `_physics_process` keeps being called at the full unscaled 60Hz rate with `delta==0.0` — Godot derives tick *count* from unscaled wall clock, only scales *delta*. Verified against the real repo: `player.gd`'s `_dodge_tick` advanced from 3 to 7 across a 0.06s freeze (burning ~31% of the 13-tick i-frame window while the game looks frozen); `combatant.gd`'s `_parry_tick_counter` dropped from 7 to 3 across the same freeze (a 0.12s hit-stop would fully expire Stone's 7-tick parry window). Motion genuinely freezes (delta-based lerp/gravity math goes inert), but **manual tick counters do not** — this is a real, silent bug if hit-stop ships without addressing it, affecting already-shipped Step 4/5 code.

2. **Resume mechanism:** CLAUDE.md 6.1's literal `get_tree().create_timer(duration, true, false, true)` verified correct (`ignore_time_scale=true` is load-bearing — without it, the timer itself freezes and never fires). But **accuracy is poor at the charter's own low end**: 0.03s measured at 0/7/18/21/23ms across 5 runs (should be ~30ms). **Recommendation: since ticks provably keep firing at exactly 60Hz during a freeze, a physics-tick countdown is more accurate AND is literally what CLAUDE.md 6.1's own parenthetical already says ("2-4 frames at 60 FPS")** — the tick-count IS the spec, the `SceneTreeTimer` is just one possible implementation of it, and Research recommends the more accurate one.

3. **Camera shake:** no conflict with Step 4's `_follow_camera_pivot()` (position-only writes never perturb `rotation_degrees`) — additive `rotation_degrees = authored_pitch + shake_offset` is safe. **Gotcha:** `FastNoiseLite`'s default `frequency=0.01` makes the shake an imperceptible drift (max per-frame delta 0.00068), and CLAUDE.md 6.2's implied small seed-offset decorrelation scheme gives Pearson r=-0.76 between pitch/yaw (shake collapses onto one axis). Verified fix: `frequency≈2.0` gives ~0.02 correlation across seed offsets or distinct `seed` values. Also: measured envelope is ±0.70, not the nominal ±1.0 — tune amplitude constants against measurement.

4. **VFX — all three buildable for real with zero art assets:**
   - **Hit-flash:** a `ShaderMaterial` with `uniform float flash_intensity` compiles/runs headless, `tween_property` on `shader_parameter/flash_intensity` works. Gotcha: `get_shader_parameter` returns `null` (not the declared default) until explicitly set once in `_ready()`. Tweens do NOT advance at `time_scale=0` by default — `Tween.set_ignore_time_scale()` exists if flash should play through the freeze.
   - **Sparks:** `GPUParticles3D` + `ParticleProcessMaterial` + a flat-colored `QuadMesh` draw pass needs zero textures, verified working. `emitting` reads back `false` one frame after a one-shot burst — tests must assert trigger/config, not the `emitting` flag.
   - **Trails:** `Trail3D` does not exist in Godot 4.7 (CLAUDE.md's hedged "e.g. Trail3D/ribbon mesh" wording is right to hedge). What exists: `RibbonTrailMesh`/`TubeTrailMesh` (particle-system draw passes, not position-history nodes) and `GPUParticles3D.trail_enabled`. A hand-rolled `ImmediateMesh` triangle-strip trail builds and works (verified, ~30 lines) but needs a real material to look like anything beyond a flat-colored ribbon.

5. **Director decisions flagged (Research's read, not a ruling):** (a) tick-countdown `HitStop` autoload vs. literal `SceneTreeTimer`; (b) how to gate Step 4/5's tick counters during a freeze — global `HitStop.is_frozen()` early-return vs. per-counter skip; (c) trail scope — real `ImmediateMesh` ribbon now vs. a stub with real trigger hooks, deferring visual polish to Step 13; (d) should hit-flash/sparks play through the freeze (feels juicier, standard genre convention) or freeze with it.

## Approach & Tradeoffs (Director sign-off)
- **Hit-stop mechanism ruling:** new `autoload/hit_stop.gd` singleton (`HitStop`), matching Steps 2/3's autoload conventions. `trigger(duration_sec: float)` sets `Engine.time_scale=0.0` and a physics-tick countdown (`roundi(duration_sec * 60)`), decremented in `HitStop._physics_process` (autoloads receive `_physics_process` same as `_unhandled_input`, established since Step 3) — NOT the literal `SceneTreeTimer`. This is a deliberate implementation deviation from CLAUDE.md 6.1's literal `get_tree().create_timer(...)` call, but is MORE faithful to the charter's own stated intent — 6.1's parenthetical explicitly frames the duration as "2-4 frames at 60 FPS," and Research proved the timer is measurably inaccurate at exactly that range while tick-counting is exact by construction (ticks provably keep firing during the freeze regardless). Logged per this project's standing convention for charter deviations (Steps 3/4/5's precedent), not silently changed. `HitStop.is_frozen() -> bool` exposed for gating.
- **Tick-counter corruption fix (retrofits Steps 4/5 — cross-cutting, requires regression testing):** add `if HitStop.is_frozen(): return` as the FIRST line of `player.gd:_physics_process` and `combatant.gd:_physics_process`. This is the cleanest fix — since hit-stop's `delta==0` already makes all delta-based math (lerp, gravity, regen) inert, the only actual corruption risk is the manual tick counters (`_dodge_tick`, `_parry_tick_counter`), and an early-return also correctly freezes InputBuffer consumption (the player shouldn't be able to act mid-freeze) as a side benefit, not just the counters. Must re-run `kinematics_test.tscn` and `combat_test.tscn` after this change to confirm no regression — this is exactly the kind of cross-file change QA should scrutinize hardest.
- **Camera shake:** `CameraTrauma` script (likely a component on the `CamPivot` node or a small autoload — Implementation's call which is cleaner given `CamPivot` isn't itself scripted yet) storing `trauma: float` (added via `add_trauma(amount)`, decaying via `Trauma(t) = clamp(Trauma(t-1) - 1.5*delta, 0, 1)` per CLAUDE.md 6.2), applying `rotation_degrees = authored_pitch + Vector3(MaxPitch*trauma^2*noise(seed1,t), MaxYaw*trauma^2*noise(seed2,t), MaxRoll*trauma^2*noise(seed3,t))` each frame on top of the pitch stored in `_ready()`. **Ruling on Research's noise gotcha:** three separate `FastNoiseLite` instances with distinct `seed` values (not small offset variations of one instance) and `frequency≈2.0` (not the default 0.01), per Research's verified fix. Amplitude constants (`MaxPitch`/`MaxYaw`/`MaxRoll`) tuned against the measured ±0.70 envelope, not the nominal ±1.0 — Implementation's judgment call within that constraint, logged in its summary.
- **VFX play through the freeze:** hit-flash Tween uses `set_ignore_time_scale()`, particle bursts are unaffected by `time_scale` regardless (physics ticks, not the particle draw itself, gate emission triggers) — juicier and standard genre convention (a flash/spark sells the impact precisely during the freeze frame, not after it). Ruling, not left to Implementation's guess.
- **Hit-flash:** `ShaderMaterial` w/ `uniform float flash_intensity : hint_range(0,1) = 0.0` applied to the placeholder `MeshInstance3D` (both `player.gd`'s and `combatant.gd`'s), explicitly set once in `_ready()` (Research's null-until-set gotcha) before any tween touches it. Triggered on `EventBus.entity_damaged` (white flash on the entity that took damage) via a small reusable helper (Implementation's call on exact wiring — e.g. a method on the placeholder-mesh owner, or a shared utility).
- **Sparks:** `GPUParticles3D` (one-shot, pooled per Step 14's budget note — reuse a small pool rather than instancing per hit, though Step 6 can ship a minimal pool of e.g. 4-8 emitters and note the full 24-emitter budget is Step 14's tuning pass) + flat-colored `QuadMesh` particle material, oriented via the hit contact normal (available from Step 5's `area_entered` collision — Implementation should thread the contact point/normal through if not already, or use the defender's position as a reasonable stand-in if exact contact-point plumbing is out of scope for this step, noting which was chosen).
- **Trails: stub with real hooks, per Research's recommendation.** A `WeaponTrail` script with real `start_trail()`/`stop_trail()` methods, called from Step 5's EXISTING `enable_hitbox()`/`disable_hitbox()` (the mechanism is already there — Step 6 just adds a subscriber), recording tip/hilt world positions into an `ImmediateMesh` triangle strip each physics frame while active. Visual polish (actual textured/tapered ribbon look) is explicitly deferred to Step 13 — this step delivers the real, testable recording mechanism (assert history length grows while active, clears/resets on stop), not a finished visual.
- **Testing:** headless test asserting (1) hit-stop actually sets `time_scale=0` for the requested tick count and restores it; (2) the tick-counter corruption fix — trigger a dodge or parry, trigger hit-stop mid-window, confirm the tick counter does NOT advance during the freeze (this is the single most important assertion in this step, directly proving the Step 4/5 retrofit works); (3) camera trauma decay matches the formula and the two/three shake axes are NOT strongly correlated (spot-check, not full Pearson analysis) at the tuned frequency/seed settings; (4) hit-flash shader parameter round-trips and tweens correctly; (5) `WeaponTrail`'s recording mechanism grows/clears correctly around `start_trail()`/`stop_trail()`. Also re-run `kinematics_test.tscn`/`combat_test.tscn`/`input_buffer_test.tscn` as regressions given the cross-cutting `_physics_process` change.

**Judgment calls (as flagged by the sign-off for Implementation's discretion):**
1. **Camera trauma decay tick:** `_physics_process`, not `_process`. Reasoning documented in the script: keeps the decay/shake math on the same delta-based convention as every other physics-tick system in this project (Step 4 kinematics, Step 5 parry ticks), and means a HitStop freeze (`delta==0`) automatically freezes the shake too, with no separate `HitStop.is_frozen()` guard needed.
2. **Shake amplitude constants:** `MaxPitch=4.0deg`, `MaxYaw=4.0deg`, `MaxRoll=2.5deg`, tuned down from a naive "few degrees" guess against Research's measured ±0.70 noise envelope (not the nominal ±1.0) so `trauma=1.0` worst-case still reads as a subtle few-degree shake, not a jarring one.
3. **Sparks — contact-point sourcing:** used the defender's `global_position + Vector3.UP*1.0` (roughly chest height) oriented along `Vector3.UP` as the documented stand-in, NOT a true contact normal — Step 5's `Area3D`-based `area_entered` resolution has no contact-point/normal data (no `KinematicCollision3D` equivalent for `Area3D` overlaps), and threading real contact-point plumbing through Step 5's collision resolution was judged out of scope for this step. Noted directly in `spark_pool.gd`'s header comment.
4. **Sparks — pool size:** 6 emitters per actor (`SparkPool.POOL_SIZE`), well under Step 14's eventual global 24-emitter budget; each `Combatant`/`Player` gets its own pool rather than one shared global pool, since neither wiring nor contention for a shared pool was specified and per-actor pools are simpler to trigger from each actor's own hit-reaction handler.
5. **Hit-stop gameplay wiring:** although not one of the 7 literally-enumerated deliverables, wired `HitStop.trigger(0.05)` on `EventBus.parry_executed` (in `combatant.gd`) to satisfy the task brief's Definition of Done ("Hit-stop actually freezes gameplay motion... on a heavy-hit/parry signal") and CLAUDE.md 6.1. Did NOT wire it to `entity_damaged`/heavy hits — the current placeholder damage system (`_resolve_incoming_hit`'s hardcoded `base_damage=20.0`) has no heavy/light attack distinction yet (that split is Step 10/13 territory), so there's no unambiguous "heavy hit" signal to hook without guessing. This is a real but conservative gap: normal (non-parry) hits currently never trigger hit-stop.
6. **Weapon trail contact-point sourcing:** tracks the hitbox `Area3D`'s own `global_position` directly (`track_node = hitbox`), not separate tip/hilt points — the task's literal wording ("recording the hitbox's world position") only mentions one point, and `combatant.tscn`'s `Weapon/Hitbox` has no separate tip/hilt markers to source from without inventing new scene nodes out of scope for this step.
7. **Hit-flash duplication:** `HIT_FLASH_SHADER`/`HIT_FLASH_DURATION`/`_play_hit_flash()` are duplicated between `player.gd` and `combatant.gd` rather than factored into a shared helper — matches this project's established convention (visible in Steps 4/5) of explicit per-file code over cross-cutting abstractions for small (~10-line) blocks.
